About ComicCraft

An end-to-end AI comic story creator powered by Gemini and generative image models.

What is ComicCraft?

ComicCraft transforms a user's story idea into a complete illustrated 5-panel comic. You provide the story idea, character, setting, tone, and art style — the AI does the rest.

Key Features

  • AI-generated 5-panel comic outlines with structured JSON validation
  • Character consistency across all panels via visual identity profiles
  • Pluggable image generation (Gemini, HuggingFace, or demo mock)
  • Per-panel regeneration without affecting other panels
  • Story continuation into new chapters with memory of previous events
  • Professional PDF export with cover page, panels, narration, dialogue
  • Comic library with favorites, search, and delete
  • Secure user authentication with bcrypt + JWT
  • Background generation jobs with real-time status polling
